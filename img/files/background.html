Where Two Worlds Collided ✨🎀

Their individual journeys before crossing paths and becoming an iconic duo.

🏝️ Palawan Roots: Jeo Ong

Raised in Puerto Princesa as part of the adventurous Ong Fam, Jeremiah 'Jeo' Ong built a grounded life centered on sports, music, and his clothing brand, Masid carrying a calm, boy-next-door charm before entering PBB: Collab 3.0.

👑 Global Stage: Gehlee Dangca

From teen beauty pageants in Las Piñas to debuting worldwide with the K-pop girl group UNIS, Gehlee Dangca brought poise, bright humor, and star power into the PBB house, winning hearts before her story began.

🎀 First Meeting: Where #JeoLee Began

Nobody expected a chill adventure boy from Palawan and a global K-pop idol from UNIS to become everyone's favorite duo—yet the moment Jeo and Gehlee shared the same room in PBB: Collab 3.0, their natural banter and protective bond stole the spotlight.
